Boyong Wu 吴博镛

Hello hello! :) I’m a graduating M.Sc. Computer Science student at the Technical University of Munich. I work on multimodal learning, computer vision and explainable AI, and my favourite part is putting the research ideas into the pipeline that runs them.

I am a student researcher in the Explainable Machine Learning group at Helmholtz Munich, with Zeynep Akata, where I am having an amazing time. My first paper was recently accepted at ECCV 2026, where we analysed how multimodal large language models process visual information throughout their layers: why and how LLM layers progressively recover visual quality, and the influence of bidirectional attention on MLLMs. I’ve also shipped software in industry, CI/CD for the Volkswagen Group In-Car App Store at CARIAD, tried building a startup called Squareflick, and assembly line engineering at Audi.

Away from that I build things for the fun of it, rendering, diffusion, VR, and small web tools for problems that annoy me.

I also like badminton, snowboarding and great food :)
